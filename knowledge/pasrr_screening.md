# PASRR

DRAFT. VERIFY BEFORE USE. State forms differ. Massachusetts uses its own Level I process.

Preadmission Screening and Resident Review (PASRR) is required before admission to a Medicaid-certified nursing facility.

- Level I is the screen for every referred person. It asks whether serious mental illness, intellectual disability, or a related condition may be present.
- Level II is the deeper evaluation when Level I flags that possibility. A nursing facility should not admit ahead of a required Level II except in the narrow exemptions the state publishes (for example some short convalescent stays).

If the record does not contain a PASRR result, the packet should say it is not documented rather than imply the screen was completed.
