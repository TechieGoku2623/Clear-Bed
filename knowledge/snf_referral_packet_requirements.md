# Referral packet contents

DRAFT. VERIFY BEFORE USE. Facilities differ. This is a typical list, not a CMS-mandated form.

A skilled nursing referral usually includes:

- History and physical, or a clearly marked placeholder when the narrative is not in structured data
- Current medication list
- Therapy notes and PT/OT evaluations when they exist
- PASRR Level I screening, and Level II when it applies
- Insurance and member identifiers
- Code status
- Isolation or equipment needs (dialysis, trach/vent, bariatric, behavioral)

If a section is not in the record, write `[NEEDS INPUT]`. Do not draft a clinical fact that the record does not contain.
