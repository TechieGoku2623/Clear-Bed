# Home services

DRAFT. VERIFY BEFORE USE.

Home health (often delivered by a visiting nurse association) is skilled care at home: nursing, PT, OT, or speech. Medicare home health generally requires the person to be homebound and to need skilled care that is ordered by a clinician.

A personal care attendant (PCA), through MassHealth, helps with daily activities. PCA services are not a substitute for skilled nursing or for a therapy plan.

Home oxygen, when it is already in the record, is a supply that has to be confirmed with a vendor and a qualifying order. Do not invent an oxygen order that is not documented.
