# Escalation playbook

DRAFT. VERIFY BEFORE USE. Local hospital policy wins when it conflicts with this note.

Post-acute placement: confirm the qualifying stay and skilled need, match facilities that accept the payer and the care needs, draft packets, and wait for a case manager to approve each send.

Payer pending: financial counseling starts or continues the application, lists the missing proofs, and does not promise a bed date.

Guardianship or capacity: social work confirms whether a health care proxy already exists. Legal is consulted before anyone discusses a court petition. Placement calls wait until decision-making authority is clear.

Home services: home health coordination confirms homebound status, skilled need, a receiving clinician, and equipment such as oxygen. A PCA referral is a separate MassHealth path.

No barrier: keep the expected discharge plan with the attending team. Do not open a nursing-facility search.

Nothing in this playbook authorizes an automatic send.
