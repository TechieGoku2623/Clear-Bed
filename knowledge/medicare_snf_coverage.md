# Medicare SNF coverage

DRAFT. VERIFY BEFORE USE against the current Medicare and CMS pages. This note is a working aid for a synthetic-data demo. It is not a coverage determination.

Traditional Medicare Part A may help pay for a skilled nursing facility (SNF) stay when all of the following are true:

- The person had a qualifying inpatient hospital stay of at least 3 consecutive days. The day of discharge does not count toward the 3 days.
- Time spent in observation, even overnight, does not count as an inpatient day.
- The SNF admission is generally within 30 days of leaving the hospital.
- A clinician orders daily skilled nursing or skilled rehabilitation.
- The facility is Medicare-certified.

Benefit-period basics, which must be re-checked before use: up to 100 days in a benefit period, with full coverage for days 1–20 and a daily coinsurance for days 21–100. Medicare Advantage plans can use different prior-authorization rules and some waive the 3-day stay. A swing bed or an interrupted stay has its own rules.

If the 3-day count is not met, say so from the hospital day count. Do not tell a family that coverage is approved.
