# MassHealth long-term care

DRAFT. VERIFY BEFORE USE with current MassHealth member guidance and the hospital's financial-counseling procedure.

MassHealth long-term-care coverage depends on both a clinical need for nursing-facility level of care and financial eligibility. An application can sit in a pending state while proofs of income, assets, and residency are reviewed.

While eligibility is pending:

- Many facilities will not accept the admission, or will accept it only under a written agreement.
- The case manager should not describe pending coverage as approved coverage.
- Financial counseling owns the application checklist: identity, insurance cards, income, assets, and any spouse-in-the-community documents the current form requires.

A patient with no payer on the stay is treated as unresolved until a coverage source is documented. That is an operational flag, not a MassHealth decision.
