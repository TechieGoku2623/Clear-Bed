# Proxy and guardianship

DRAFT. VERIFY BEFORE USE with the hospital's legal and social-work policy. This is not legal advice.

A health care proxy is the document an adult signs to name someone to make health care decisions if they later cannot. It is the preferred path when the person still has capacity to sign.

Guardianship is a court process used when the person cannot make informed decisions and no adequate proxy or other authority is in place. It takes longer than a placement call. A clinician does not "appoint" a guardian at the bedside.

When dementia and living alone are both present, the copilot escalates to social work and legal. It does not file a petition and it does not claim a guardian has been appointed.
